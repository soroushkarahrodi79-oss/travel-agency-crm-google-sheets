# Code of conduct

We want a technically rigorous and welcoming project.

Participants must:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on the work;
- welcome people regardless of background or experience;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination and publication of private data;
- disclose conflicts of interest when reviewing contributions;
- prioritize user safety when discussing customer or payment information.

Project maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that violate these
rules and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

Report conduct issues through a private GitHub channel rather than a public
issue when the report contains personal information.
